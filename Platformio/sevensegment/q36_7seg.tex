\section{Boolean Simplification}

Given,

[
[X+Z{\bar Y+(\bar Z+X\bar Y)}]
[\bar X+\bar Z(X+Y)]
]

For (X=1),

[
F=\bar Z
]

For output 1,

[
Z=0
]
